# Persona Infographic Image Rules

This file captures the image-generation bias for the preferred infographic-toon style.

## Core Mood
- dark editorial
- cinematic but information-dense
- strategy memo + emergency bulletin + illustrated scene card

## Composition Bias
- left-heavy hero composition
- right-side stacked cards
- footer closer
- asymmetry preferred over evenly tiled grids

## Card Types
- `hero-scene`: largest illustration-led block
- `paper-card`: light background comparison/checklist/table shell
- `warning-card`: red or black urgency block
- `chain-card`: 1-2-3 escalation or process strip
- `closing-strip`: bottom strategic message

## Baked Text Guidance
- Safe:
  - short headers
  - numbers
  - stamps
  - badges
  - 1 short line captions
- Unsafe:
  - full body paragraphs
  - dense exact tables
  - multi-line Korean checklist prose

## Prompt Phrases That Usually Help
- `asymmetrical editorial intelligence-brief poster`
- `dark cinematic infographic-toon`
- `stacked dossier cards`
- `off-white paper comparison panel`
- `red warning stamp overlay`
- `executive risk briefing atmosphere`

## Prompt Phrases To Avoid
- `clean modern dashboard`
- `startup landing page infographic`
- `minimal swiss poster`
- `cute manga panel page`
- `pastel app presentation`
